What affects your final price?

  • Property size — square footage and number of rooms.
  • Age & condition — peeling or chipping paint and multiple paint layers.
  • Accessibility — attics, crawl spaces, and outbuildings.
  • Exterior factors — bare soil and play areas.
  • Renovation history — prior work that may have disturbed lead paint.
  • Unit count — multi-unit and portfolio properties receive volume pricing.
